# Code of conduct

This is a research-mapping project. The norms are simple and they are about the work.

## The standard

- **Skepticism is welcome and wanted; contempt is not.** Critique bricks, not people.
- **A found break is a success.** Refuting a claim (including the maintainers' own) improves the
  map. Bring the counterexample, the failed check, or the gap, in the open, with the artifact in
  hand.
- **Label evidence honestly.** Never present numerical evidence as proof. "I could not verify
  this" is a valuable contribution.
- **Credit accurately.** Cite prior work and fellow contributors; do not claim others' bricks.
- **Stay on scope.** This atlas is pure mathematics about MUB(6). Keep discussion there.

## Not acceptable

Personal attacks, harassment, demeaning or discriminatory comments, deliberate
misrepresentation of someone's claim, or bad-faith disruption. Gatekeeping by credential rather
than by argument is also off-limits: a correct, checkable brick from a newcomer counts exactly
as much as one from an expert.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and comments that violate this code, and may
block repeat or bad-faith offenders. Report concerns by opening an issue marked `conduct` or by
emailing the maintainer at noah@invariantsystems.io. Reports are handled discreetly.

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, trimmed to a research context.
