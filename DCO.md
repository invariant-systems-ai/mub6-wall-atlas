# Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions to the MUB(6) Wall Atlas are accepted under the **Developer Certificate of
Origin 1.1** (the same mechanism used by the Linux kernel and many research projects). It is a
lightweight, per-commit affirmation, not a copyright assignment. You keep authorship of your
bricks.

## How to sign

Add a `Signed-off-by` line to each commit (git does this with `git commit -s`):

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

By doing so you certify the statement below.

## Inbound license

Unless you state otherwise in your contribution, you agree that your contribution is licensed
under the **same terms as this project**: Apache License 2.0 for code, CC BY 4.0 for documents.
Apache-2.0 includes an explicit patent license from you covering your contribution, which is
why we use it: it keeps the provenance of every brick clean and unencumbered for everyone who
reuses the atlas.

If your contribution lives in your own repository (a Sage/Julia/Lean reimplementation, say), a
short note in your PR confirming it is offered under Apache-2.0 / CC BY 4.0 is sufficient.
